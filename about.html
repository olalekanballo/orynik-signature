About Orynik Signature

Enhancing your natural beauty, one glam at a time.

Makeup that starts with you

Orynik Signature is a makeup studio in Ibadan. We do birthday glam, owambe, wedding glam and soft glam, and we travel to clients who prefer to get ready at home.

Our approach is simple. We enhance what is already there. The skin tone, the features and the personality stay yours, and the makeup brings them forward.

We also train new artists, and every step of the work is explained as we go.
