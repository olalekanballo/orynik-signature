Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you book.

How do I book?

Open the booking page, choose your service, date and time, and send the request. You will then confirm the details with us on WhatsApp.

Do you accept students for training?

Yes. Training is hands-on, and every step is explained as it is done so you understand the reason behind it. Message us on WhatsApp to ask about the next available class.

Do you offer home service?

Yes. Choose home service on the booking page and enter your address. We will confirm the visit with you on WhatsApp.

Which occasions do you cover?

Birthday glam, owambe, wedding glam and soft glam. If your event is something else, send us a message and we will tell you if we can help.

How early should I book?

As early as you can, especially for weekends and wedding season. Your date is only held once we confirm it with you.

How should I prepare for my appointment?

Arrive with a clean, moisturised face. Send us your outfit colours and any inspiration pictures, and tell us about any skin allergies.

How do I pay, and what if I need to change my date?

We agree payment details with you on WhatsApp when we confirm your booking. If your plans change, message us as early as possible so we can find a new date.

Where are you located?

Araromi Estate, Ababaale, Olunde, Ibadan. You can also find us on TikTok @orynik0.
