How booking works

  1. Send your request

    Choose a service, date and time on the booking page. Tell us if you want home service.

  2. Confirm on WhatsApp

    We reply to agree the details, answer your questions and confirm your slot.

  3. Get glammed

    Come to Araromi Estate, or we arrive at your home, and we get you ready for your day.

Before your appointment

A little preparation helps your makeup sit better and last longer.

  • Arrive with a clean, moisturised face.
  • Send us a photo or the colour of your outfit and head tie.
  • Bring inspiration pictures of looks you like.
  • Tell us about any skin allergies before we begin.
  • Book early for weekends and wedding season so your date stays free.
